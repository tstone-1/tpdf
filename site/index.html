A fast PDF viewer and editor for macOS and Windows

Free and open source. No account, no telemetry, and your documents stay on your computer.

Download for MacApple silicon, signed and notarized Download for Windows64-bit, no administrator rights needed

On a Mac you can also run brew install --cask tstone-1/tpdf/tpdf. There is no build for Intel Macs, Windows on ARM or Linux.

The Windows installer is code-signed; the publisher reads Open Source Developer Timo Stein. The certificate is new, so Windows can show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: choose More info, check the publisher, then Run anyway.

Remove personal information before you share a file

A redaction in tpdf removes the words from the file. It does not draw a black box over them.

  1. Open the command palette and type redact.
  2. Drag over the text or the region to remove. Every region is listed for review.
  3. Choose Redact and save as. tpdf reads the result back and tells you whether the removal is verified.

For a document somebody else redacted, Find text the pages do not show lists the words that are still in the file.

Sign a document, and check a signature you received

Sign with a certificate from the macOS keychain or the Windows certificate store, with a timestamp if you want one. Or draw your signature once and place the image on any page.

For a signed document, tpdf says whether each signature is intact, whether your computer trusts the signer, and what was added after signing.

Mark up, fill in and rearrange

Highlight, underline, draw, add text boxes, stamps and comments. Fill forms and create form fields. Turn, move, delete, crop, extract and insert pages; merge documents or split one.

It also makes a scanned document searchable, saves a smaller copy, and sets or removes a password.
